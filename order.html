    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few notes before you send anything.

    Is the calculator quote final?

    No. It's a ballpark based on area and the options you select. We confirm real pricing after reviewing your actual files or requirements.

    What counts as "finding an IC" on a PCB order?

    If you know a chip you want, that's free — we just design around it. If you only know what you need it to do and leave the part selection to us, that's a sourcing surcharge for main ICs. Passives (resistors, capacitors, etc.) are always sourced for free.

    What do I actually receive from a PCB design order?

    Bill of materials, Gerbers, drill files, and schematic — everything you need to get the board manufactured and assembled wherever you like. We design the board; we don't manufacture it.

    What file do you need for a stencil order?

    A Gerber for the paste (stencil) layer of your board. If you're not sure which layer that is, send the full Gerber set and we'll pull it.
