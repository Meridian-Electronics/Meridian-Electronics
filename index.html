// 01 — About

Who We Are

A small electronics company built to remove the tedious part of hardware development.

We are a small electronics company out of Connecticut, and our goal is simple. We understand all the challenges that come from cheap knockoff chips, unlabeled breakout boards, and modules with no documentation. We want to get rid of all of those challenges, with high-quality, easy-to-understand, documented, tested, impeccable products.

Why should an inventor spend days, even weeks, finding out how to build a USB-C host, when we can do that for them? They do the hard stuff. We do the tedious stuff.

We are built for hobbyists and innovators alike. Every module ships with a real datasheet — pinouts, example code, debugging notes, the works — because a board without documentation is just a mystery you paid for.

Thank you for shopping Meridian Electronics.
